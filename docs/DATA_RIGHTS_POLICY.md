---

## 1. Statutory Provenance & Open Data Framework

Devyatra ingests cultural and geographic data from statutory authorities under explicit legal frameworks:
1. **National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP)**:
   - Data sourced from the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) and the Ministry of Culture is utilized under Open Government Data (OGD) principles.
2. **Statutory Shrine Board Notices**:
   - Official darshan timings and seva booking notices from autonomous shrine boards (TTD, SMVDSB, Kashi Vishwanath) are republished as public notices for pilgrim welfare.
3. **Google Places Platform**:
   - Discovered places data is queried and attributed in accordance with Google Maps Platform Terms of Service. Places data is refreshed every 30 days and never permanently retained without attribution.

---

## 2. Quarantined & Unknown Rights

Any data source not explicitly categorized in `SOURCE_RIGHTS_REGISTRY` is assigned `RIGHTS_UNKNOWN`. It cannot be utilized for commercial publications or high-resolution reproduction until a formal legal review is completed.
