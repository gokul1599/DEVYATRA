## 1. Geographic Gap Distribution

While all 36 States and Union Territories have representative temples, 203 official administrative districts remain to be indexed. The majority of unrepresented districts fall into three distinct geographic profiles:

1. **North-Eastern Hill Districts**: Arunachal Pradesh (e.g. Kra Daadi, Dibang Valley), Nagaland (Noklak, Tuensang), Mizoram, and Manipur frontier subdivisions where Hindu shrines are historically concentrated in district capitals.

3. **High-Altitude Himalayan Wilderness**: Frontier administrative tracts in Ladakh and Himachal Pradesh where permanent monastic or temple structures are clustered around river valleys.
