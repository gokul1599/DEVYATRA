## 1. Privacy Policy Summary
Devyatra operates on a strict zero-PII, zero-ad-tracking privacy foundation. We do not sell user data, capture sensitive financial credentials, or track pilgrims across external websites. Anonymous session IDs rotate daily.

## 2. Terms of Service Summary
Devyatra provides verified cultural, geographic, and pilgrimage directory data as a public service. Users agree to use pilgrimage planning tools for lawful personal travel and research. Automated high-frequency scraping of the platform without authorization is prohibited.

## 3. Cookie Policy Summary
Devyatra uses only essential first-party cookies necessary for platform operation:
- `tem_lang`: Stores user language preference (one of 12 Indic vernacular options).
- `tem_session`: Ephemeral session state for authenticated administrators.
Zero third-party advertising or tracking cookies are utilized.

## 4. Data Attribution Policy
All temple metadata, epigraphical history, and coordinates derived from government registries (ASI, HR&CE, State Tourism) carry explicit statutory citations on their respective pages.

## 5. Source & Content Policy
We strictly distinguish documented archaeological history from living sacred belief and oral tradition. We do not arbitrate theological disputes or canonical sectarian affiliations.

## 6. Community Submission Policy
Yatri feedback and correction submissions are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0). Submissions pass through our 6-stage moderation pipeline and never directly overwrite authoritative records.

## 7. AI Use & AI Information Disclaimer
The Devyatra AI companion and itinerary planner utilize grounded generative AI constrained by verified database facts. Because temple timings, road access, and weather can change abruptly due to administrative or natural factors, yatris must confirm final arrangements with official shrine boards.

## 8. Media Rights Policy
All media assets are audited for licensing provenance. Unverified media is quarantined.

## 9. Map Provider Attribution Policy
Interactive mapping layers utilize Leaflet and open cartographic data with full attribution to OpenStreetMap contributors and Google Maps Platform where applicable.
