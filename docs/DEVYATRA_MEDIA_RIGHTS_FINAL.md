## 1. Media Assets Governance

All visual and audio assets in Devyatra operate under explicit licensing categories:
1. `OFFICIAL`: Directly authorized by temple devasthanams.
2. `GOVERNMENT`: Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) and Open Government Data (OGD) public archives.
3. `LICENSED`: Licensed editorial photography.
4. `PUBLIC_DOMAIN`: Historical works where copyright has expired.
5. `UNKNOWN_RIGHTS`: Quarantined from public serving until rights verification is complete.
