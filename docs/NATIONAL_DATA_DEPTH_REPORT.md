## 2. Technical District Depth Scoring Model

To avoid political or devotional bias, the Devyatra Data Depth Score measures **dataset completeness and auditability**, not cultural or spiritual importance.

| Dimension | Points | Description |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Source Provenance** | 25 | Proportion of records with identified statutory or scholarly provenance |
| **Verification Level** | 25 | Verified by official board, HR&CE, or field survey vs community reported |
| **Operational Schedules** | 20 | Explicit morning/evening darshan slots, daily aarti, and festival calendars |
| **Statutory Booking Clarity** | 15 | Documented booking modes (free, offline counter, or statutory portal URL) |
| **Vernacular & Geocoding** | 15 | Native Indic names recorded alongside high-precision coordinates |
| **Total Completeness Score** | **100** | Pure data completeness metric |

## 3. Data Tiering Distribution

- **Tier A (Deep Data, ≥ 75 pts)**: Complete operational timings, statutory portal links, native Indic names, high-precision geocodes, and documented history. Includes Tirupati, Kashi, Madurai, Puri, Ujjain, Thanjavur, Dwarka.
- **Tier B (Moderate Data, 50–74 pts)**: Coordinates, deity, administrative parentage, and verified source; seasonal or basic timing slots recorded.
- **Tier C (Foundational Data, 25–49 pts)**: Confirmed existence and geocodes backed by Census/LGD administrative mapping; timings pending direct devasthanam review.
- **Tier D (Sparse Data, < 25 pts)**: Initial discovery records requiring secondary source enrichment.
